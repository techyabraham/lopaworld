# Owner inputs and open decisions

## Status of the visual identity proposal

Reviewed 10 October 2026. The supplied proposal labels itself a concept direction for review and says its colours, logo, campaign lines and images need Lopaworld approval before production use. Its recommendations are recorded here as proposals, not approved brand facts. Do not replace the implementation tokens or publish the copy below until the owner approves it.

The proposal recommends this shared palette:

| Page background | Lopa Ivory | `#F5F1E9` |

| Borders and quiet panels | Soft Stone | `#D8D1C7` |

It proposes label accents for Lopa Next (`#6A2638`), Lopa Trybe (`#28564A`), Lopa Prime (`#27364A`), Lopa Men (`#A3543E`), Lopafashion (`#60405A`) and Lopaworld (`#A98252`). It does not give Lopa Bridal an accent. Contrast still needs testing against the final use cases before adoption.

The logo direction is a custom `LOPA` wordmark with `WORLD` beneath, a compact `L` monogram and related label lockups. No logo artwork is embedded in the proposal. It suggests the campaign platform “The Lopa Entrance,” with “Make your entrance,” “Dress like you mean to be remembered,” and the public phrase “Look Important. Be Important.” as candidate lines. These are not approved assets or copy.

The proposal's label positioning and category suggestions are research leads: Next for couture/elegance, Trybe for contemporary nativewear, Prime for bespoke suits, Men for casualwear and accessories, Fashion for women's fashion, and Bridal as a possible label. It also suggests Lagos city fashion-editorial imagery, label-specific campaign directions, and responsive campaign/product asset sets. Product-level assignments, image files, usage rights and final copy are not supplied.

## Scope reconciliation

The master build brief fixes seven destinations and explicitly includes Lopa Bridal as a normal label. The proposal calls the system six labels in several places and says Bridal scope needs confirmation. Keep the seven-label project scope unless the owner changes the master brief. Bridal still needs its visual treatment: proposed accent, logo lockup, campaign direction and approved copy. The proposal also uses `Lopafashion`, while the master brief names the destination `Lopa Fashion`; confirm the public spelling and display name.
